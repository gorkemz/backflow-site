1. What Testmark is

Testmark is a record-keeping and calculation aid for certified backflow prevention assembly testers. It stores your testers, gauges, customers, sites and assemblies; records the readings you take; computes a suggested pass or fail result from those readings; produces a printable PDF report; and reminds you about retest, calibration and certification dates.

2. It does not replace your professional judgment

This is the most important paragraph on this page, so it is not buried at the bottom.

Testmark is a tool. You are the tester. The app does not inspect an assembly, cannot see the gauge, and cannot verify that a number you typed is the number you read. Its pass/fail calculation is an assistant: it applies arithmetic to the values you enter. It is not a determination, not a certification, and not a second opinion from a qualified person.

The test result, the report you sign, and everything that follows from filing it remain your professional and legal responsibility. Your signature on a report is your statement, not ours. Before you sign or submit anything the app produced:

  • check every reading against what your gauge actually showed;
  • check the result against the standards and codes you are certified under;
  • check that the form, the fields and the submission method match what the water purveyor receiving it currently requires.

Requirements differ by state, by purveyor and by year, and they change without telling software vendors. We do not guarantee that the app’s report layout, its fields, or its checks match the requirements that apply to your work today. Where the app and your certifying authority, your code, or your purveyor disagree, they are right and the app is wrong.

Nothing in the app is legal, regulatory, or engineering advice, and using it does not create a professional relationship between us.
