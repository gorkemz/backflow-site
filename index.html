Test reports that hold up, from the mechanical room.

Backflow is an iPhone app for certified backflow prevention assembly testers. Enter the readings, get a clean report, and stop keeping retest dates in your head.

What it does

  • Records the test. Readings, checklist answers, repairs and remarks, with the tester name, certification number and gauge serial captured as they were on the day of the test.
  • Does the arithmetic. A suggested pass or fail as you type, so a transposed digit shows up while you are still standing at the assembly—not after the report is filed.
  • Produces a PDF. Print it, email it, or file it with the purveyor. Tester and customer signatures are drawn on the phone and stamped in a fixed place on the page.
  • Watches three clocks. Assembly retest dates, gauge calibration expiry, and your own certification renewal, each with lead time to actually do something about it.
  • Works offline. No connection needed to record a test or make a report. Mechanical rooms do not have signal.
  • Keeps your book of business yours. Records live on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud account. There is no account with us and no server of ours.

Export is free, permanently

A full JSON archive, a CSV of every report, and individual report PDFs are available without a subscription and always will be. The app also keeps rolling local snapshots of your records so a mistaken delete is recoverable. Software that holds your filing history hostage does not deserve your filing history.

The app assists. You sign.

The pass/fail calculation applies arithmetic to the numbers you enter. It does not inspect the assembly and cannot check your gauge. The determination, the signature and the submission stay your professional responsibility—and what your state and water purveyor require can differ from what any app prints.
