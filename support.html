Support

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Contact

mail@gorkemerbektas.com

One person reads this address. We aim to reply within a few business days. Email is the only support channel—there is no phone line and no chat, and we would rather say so than have you wait for one.

What to include

Because of how the app is built, we cannot see your data—not your reports, not your customers, not your device. Everything we know about a problem has to come from your message. The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • What you were doing, step by step, right before it went wrong.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • The app version and your iOS version (Settings › General › About).
  • Your iPhone model.
  • A screenshot, if there is something to see. Blank out customer details first if you would rather not send them—we do not need them to reproduce most bugs.

The app’s built-in “contact support” links open your mail app with some of this already filled in.

What we can help with

  • Bugs and crashes. Wrong results, a screen that will not load, a PDF that comes out wrong, a reminder that did not fire.
  • Getting your data out. How to export a full JSON archive, a CSV of your reports, or a single report PDF. Export is free and is not behind a subscription.
  • Recovering from a mistake. The app keeps the ten most recent local snapshots of your records. If you deleted something you should not have, write before you keep working—new saves roll older snapshots off the end.
  • Sync questions. Why records are or are not appearing on a second device.
  • Report and form questions. What a field means, or why a value is calculated the way it is.
  • Feature requests. Genuinely wanted. Tell us what the job is, not just the feature—the underlying problem is usually the useful part.

What we cannot do

  • We cannot retrieve your records. They are on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud database. We have no copy, no access, and no administrative view. This is deliberate—see the Privacy Policy—but it means data recovery has to happen on your end, and we can only guide you through it.
  • We cannot issue refunds. App Store purchases are refunded by Apple. Use reportaproblem.apple.com. Tell us anyway if something was broken.
  • We cannot cancel your subscription. Do it in Settings › your Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • We cannot tell you whether a test passed. The app calculates a suggested result from the readings you enter; the determination and the signature are yours. See section 2 of the Terms of Use.
  • We cannot advise on code or regulation. What your state or water purveyor requires is between you, your certifying authority, and them.

Common questions

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, iOS also syncs it to the private database in your own Apple Account, which is how it reaches your other devices. There is no account with us and no server of ours involved.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to record a test. Entering readings, calculating a result, and producing a PDF all work offline—which matters in a mechanical room. A connection is needed for iCloud sync to catch up and for subscription status to refresh.

How do I back up my records?

Export a JSON archive from the sharing screen and keep it somewhere you control. The app also writes rolling local snapshots automatically, but those live on the same device, so they do not help if the phone does.

One caveat: the JSON archive carries your records, but not the drawn signature images or the stored copies of generated PDFs. If a signed PDF matters to you, export that report’s PDF as well.

I deleted the app. Is my data gone?

The on-device copy, including local snapshots, is removed with the app. If iCloud sync was on, a copy may still be in your iCloud account and can come back when you reinstall. Do not rely on that—export before deleting.

The app opened on a recovery screen. What now?

That screen appears when the app could not open its stored records. It is deliberate: the app refuses to start over with an empty database, because that is how records get quietly lost. Your file is left untouched on disk. Do not delete the app—write to us, and include what the screen says.

Why did a reminder not arrive?

First check that notifications are allowed for Testmark in iOS Settings, and that Focus or a Sleep schedule is not silencing them. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device, so they do not depend on a connection. If permissions look right and reminders are still missing, tell us which date you expected.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel in Settings › your Apple Account › Subscriptions. Refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
